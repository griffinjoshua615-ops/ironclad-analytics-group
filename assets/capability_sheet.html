What Dokimos does

Dokimos writes down the judgment that is not in the manual, as tasks with exact answers, and proves it. The product is the ground truth AI does not have: the calls an experienced PLL clerk, dispatcher or maintenance sergeant makes that no written policy contains. Every item ships with one exact answer, a deterministic verifier, and the record of which blind solvers failed it.

The protocol

Elicit

Pull the cases out of the people who carry the judgment, one decision at a time.

Author

One exact answer per task and a verifier that needs no human in the loop.

Probe

Blind solvers run first. Anything cleared on rules alone goes back.

Prove

Exact grading, pass rate and probe log delivered with every item.
