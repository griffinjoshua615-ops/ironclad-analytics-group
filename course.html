03 · Dokimos Course

PLL and Class IX readiness judgment.

A public, scheduled, two-day class for PLL clerks, maintenance sergeants and the leaders who sign for the readiness report. Taught by people who ran the shop, from the mistakes they already made. Not a GCSS-Army buttons class.

Join the schedule list

What is taught

  1. Reading the open requisition list like a clerk with five years in. Dead, duplicate, superseded, and the status codes that lie.
  2. Supply-down or sitting on the shelf. Receipt posting, part-number supersession, and the cases where the truck is down for a paperwork reason.
  3. Expedite, cross-level or wait. Parts, funds and mission priority in one decision, with the cases that make it hard.
  4. Shop stock that earns its space. Demand history, the lines that never move, and what to turn in.
  5. The Monday meeting. Turning all of it into an action list the commander can act on in ten minutes.

Every lesson is built from adjudicated cases in the Dokimos library, with the answer and the reason. Students leave with the case book.
