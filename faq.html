Questions, answered plainly

What buyers ask before the first call. If yours is not here, ask it.

What does Dokimos do?+

Dokimos writes down the judgment that is not in the manual, as tests with exact answers, and proves it. The output is three things: expert-authored tasks and environments for AI labs (Dokimos Environments), a decision tool for Army motor pools and fleets (Dokimos Engine), and a public class for PLL clerks and maintenance sergeants (Dokimos Course).

What is a judgment task?+

A single decision an experienced person makes that no written policy fully covers, written as a problem with one exact answer, a verifier that checks it automatically, and a short reason the crux is judgment rather than a rule. Example: deciding that an open requisition is dead because the part was superseded and received under a new number.

Why do AI labs buy this?+

Frontier models now solve most tasks that have a written rule. They still fail tasks whose answer lives in an expert's head. Labs pay for ground truth in exactly those domains, and they pay more when the tasks survive a strict grader. On an independent benchmark program in late 2026, frontier models solved the founder's written-rule task in every trial and his Class IX judgment task in zero of five.

What is the probe log?+

The record of which blind solvers were run against a task before delivery and what they returned. If a model clears a task on written rules alone, the task goes back to the bench. Buyers see the gap before they pay for it.

What domains do you cover?+

Class IX parts judgment (dead, duplicate and superseded requisitions, shop stock, turn-ins), dispatch and 5988-E fault flow, deadline and NMC recovery under parts and funds constraints, forward maintenance and expeditionary supply, and warehouse and sort operations. New domains are added through veteran and industrial co-authors under the same protocol.

Do you use real Army data?+

No. Environments are built from synthetic and sanitized records that reproduce the judgment. The Engine runs offline on a unit's own laptop; nothing is uploaded, and Dokimos never asks a unit to send its exports.

How is Dokimos different from a data labeling vendor?+

Labeling vendors recruit people to annotate what a lab already has. Dokimos authors the cases, writes the exact answers and the verifiers, runs the probes, and delivers the proof. The product is verified judgment, not labor.

What does the Engine actually produce?+

An action list for the Monday maintenance meeting: which requisitions are dead, duplicated or already filled under a superseding part number; which vehicles are truly supply-down; which parts to expedite or cross-level. Every rule in it passed a test a clerk wrote.

How can a unit buy the course?+

The course is public, regularly scheduled and priced the same for everyone, $1,250 per seat, eight seats per class. That structure is meant for Government Purchase Card buying with an SF-182. Confirm with your resource manager and GPC billing official.
