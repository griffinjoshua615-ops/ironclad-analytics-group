On an independent benchmark program (September 24 to October 3, 2026), frontier AI models solved a written-rule accrual task in every automated trial and failed the founder's Class IX stockage judgment task in zero of five. That gap is what Dokimos sells: the judgment that is not written down anywhere a model can read, written down and proven.

How a test earns the streak

The gold streak on a touchstone is the mark that the metal was tested. Every Dokimos deliverable carries it, and it means the same four things happened.

  1. Step 1

    Elicit

    Sit with the people who carry the judgment and pull the cases out of their heads, one decision at a time.

  2. Step 2

    Author

    Write each case as a task with one exact answer and a verifier that checks it without a human in the loop.

  3. Step 3

    Probe

    Run blind solvers against it. Anything a model clears on rules alone goes back to the bench; that is a rule, not judgment.

  4. Step 4

    Prove

    Grade exactly, record the pass rate and the probe log, and deliver with the streak. Field outcomes become the next tests.
